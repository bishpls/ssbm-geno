# Aerial and move shapes (measured)

Units from the fighter's position (facing right), times ModelScale. Low = spheres reaching within 4 units of the feet. Low back is crossup coverage (hitting a shield or body as he lands behind it); sweep is where the hit sits on its first active frame, then its last (+ in front, - behind). Bair is measured as the game places it (behind: negative front).
