# Geno: source material and fan expectations

Research brief for adding Geno to Super Smash Bros. Melee and making his reveal trailer. Compiled 2026-09-27.
Markers: **[unverified]** means not confirmed against a primary or well-cited source. **[inference]** means our reading, not a sourced claim.

## Key takeaways

- Geno is a star spirit (♡♪!?) inside a wooden toy. The doll is the identity: jointed wood, fists that fly off, gun barrels in the forearms, a body that folds into a cannon. A Mii Gunner costume can't show that; a fighter can.
- Kit: five specials (Beam, Boost, Whirl, Blast, Flash) and six weapons, all fired from his own body.
- Signature mechanic: the **timed hit**. Release on the third star (Beam, Blast, Flash), press before contact (Whirl crits for 9999), press before the last arrow fades (Boost).
- Fastest party member, high attack, low defense: a glass cannon.
- Leitmotif: "Beware of Forest Mushrooms" (Yoko Shimomura), used by Nintendo in both costume trailers. Story cue: "Geno's Awakening".
- **No public Melee Geno seems to exist (medium confidence).** The most complete Geno is Legacy XP's (Brawl/Project M, 2016–19), which already uses the obvious faithful layout: Beam neutral, Whirl side, cannon up-B, Blast down, Flash Final Smash, timed hits everywhere. That is the prior art to differ from.
- **Melee has no Final Smash**, and every fan design uses Geno Flash as one. It needs a new home in the kit or the trailer.

## 1. Identity

**Name.** His real name is written ♡♪!? (full-width ♡♪！？ in the remake). It is "hard to pronounce", so he takes the name of the doll he inhabits. The doll belongs to Gaz, a boy at the Rose Town inn, and is his favourite of four (Mario, Peach, Bowser, Geno). The Japanese title of the doll-play music, ねぇねぇジーノごっこしようよ ("Hey, let's play Geno make-believe"), suggests Geno is a toy hero in Gaz's games; nobody recognises him as a real person.

**Story.** Smithy's giant sword Exor shatters the Star Road, the path that carries wishes, and its seven Star Pieces scatter. In Rose Town, Gaz plays too rough and fires the Geno doll's "Super Star Shot", knocking Mario out. That night a star possesses the doll "because it appeared to be the strongest of the four". It struggles to walk and **slams into a wall** on the way out, a gift for animation. After beating Bowyer in the Forest Maze with Mario and Mallow, he explains: "I serve… a higher authority…" and joins them to recover the Star Pieces. In the ending he leaves the doll, returns to star form and repairs the Star Road; the lifeless doll sits behind the party on Vista Hill. His star form is a glowing point early on and a classic yellow Mario star ringed with dots at the end.

**Personality.** Official profile: "Confident, stoic and wise, he has been sent by a higher authority to restore peace to the world." Super Mario Wiki calls him a sage-like adviser (he talks Bowser into staying) who is heroic, enthusiastic, friendly and dutiful, and who worries about the "ownership" of his body and promises to return it. Scrapbook (2023): "I hate to leave Gaz behind, but I'll need this body…" The 1996 US manual sells him as "A new hero who totes a mean gun and wears a stylin' cape!"; Nintendo in 2023 as "A guardian of Star Road sworn to protect the world's wishes with his advanced firepower. He's also a doll (kinda)." Before release, Square called him "Pinocchio" (Nintendo Power, per Super Mario Wiki).

**Design credits.** Yuka Miyamoto and Hideo Minaba designed him, with concept work by Kazuyuki Kurashima, whose early concept (published 2020) was a taller armoured knight with a sword. Minaba chose a doll because "dolls can perform actions humans cannot", balancing Mario's cuteness with cool (Wikipedia, citing Minaba's artbook).

**Ownership.** Square developed SMRPG for Nintendo (1996); ArtePiazza developed the remake, published by Nintendo (2023). **Square Enix holds Geno's copyright.** *Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga*'s credits: "the copyright of Character 'Geno', reserved by SQUARE ENIX CO., LTD." That cameo was cut from the 3DS remake, presumably over licensing.

**Smash history.**
- After SSB64, a Nintendo Japan poll had Geno tied with Mallow as the 8th most-requested Mario character (SmashWiki).
- **Brawl.** Sakurai to *Nintendo Dream* (Source Gaming translation, Feb 2016): "To tell the truth, it's because I wanted Geno to be a playable character. He has a gun for a hand, and I think he fits in really well with Smash. During Brawl, I thought it would be great if I could add him, but in the end it didn't become a reality." And: "When talking about older characters, Geno always gets a lot of requests. He didn't appear as a fighter, but I was able to achieve something kind of close to that as a Mii Costume." No reason given. Press claims he was planned for "Brawl and 4" (EventHubs, 2018) overreach: **the quote names only Brawl.** Licensing is the usual guess, weakened by Square Enix's Cloud (2015) and Sephiroth (2020).
- **Smash 3DS/Wii U.** Geno hat and outfit for the Mii Gunner, DLC in December 2015 alongside Cloud, added "due to an agreement reached between Square Enix and Nintendo" (Sakurai). The Geno Beam arm cannon sits on the right hand. Trailer music: "Beware of Forest Mushrooms".
- **Ultimate.** The costume returned (revealed 17 Dec 2020 in the Sephiroth presentation, out 22 Dec). The trailer used Living Room (Rose Town inn), Yoshi's Island (Bandit's Way) and Garden of Hope (Forest Maze), with the same music. Sakurai tweeted a Culex-battle homage (Sephiroth vs Mario, Bowser and a Geno Mii). There is a Legend-class Geno spirit. Fans read the costume as a rejection (Newsweek); ScreenRant called it "a budget Halloween costume".
- **Campaigns.** #Geno4Smash (SmashBoards support thread and Twitter). A 2019 ballot by Sabi, PapaGenos and Scott the Woz (about 6,500 participants) ranked Geno first for popularity. August 2020 polls on PapaGeno's Forest Maze Discord had him first for "most wanted" (265 votes to Shantae's 146). A December 2020 event named "Beware of Mushrooms" in Japanese sparked speculation. Also the 2019 "Operation Starfall" petition, and artist @Thinginator90's daily Geno drawings (about 1,000) until the remake's June 2023 announcement.

## 2. Abilities

**Stats as characterisation.** Geno joins at level 6 with HP 45, Speed 30, Attack 60, Defense 23, Magic Attack 25 and Magic Defense 17 (Player's Guide, via Super Mario Wiki). He has the party's highest speed and attack comparable to Bowser, but low defense. His HP growth stalls after level 20, and his best gear (Star Gun, Star Cape) is weak. Read that as **fast, hard-hitting and fragile.** Legacy XP read it the same way ("still somewhat of a glass cannon").

**How timed hits work.** On a weapon attack, a close press adds 50% damage and a perfect press doubles it. Single-press specials gain 25% (close) or 50% (perfect). In the remake a perfect press also sends a shockwave that hits every enemy, and successful presses build an Action Gauge and a chain counter.

The brief asked about an **"Arm Cannon"**, but no weapon by that name exists. Super Mario Wiki's weapon category lists only the weapons above. The **2023 model sheet** shows the rig: fists and gun-barrel forearms with gold muzzles come apart, so a hand unplugs to reveal a barrel.

**What changed in the 2023 remake.** Fully 3D with felt and wood-grain materials; doll proportions made consistent; Whirl became a buzzsaw; Flash needs 5 stars; new Stella 023 weapon and a scrapbook; Shimomura's new orchestrations, with a Classic toggle. Geno's three **Triple Moves**:
- **Star Riders** (Mario, Mallow, Geno): they ride rainbow paths made by the cyan Star Piece and fire down at one enemy (Geno fires a Geno Beam) for a starry explosion, with the Star Road behind them.
- **Shooting Star Shot** (Mario, Bowser, Geno): Bowser throws Mario and Geno skyward, Mario boosts Geno higher, and Geno fires five random shots and buffs the party for three turns.
- **Spare-Us-All** (Mario, Geno, Peach): Peach's enlarged parasol shields the party from one attack.

**Poses [unverified].** The official 2023 art shows two canonical stances: stoic with arms crossed, and a relaxed three-quarter stance with fists low and a heavy-lidded sideways glance. We did not check victory, defeat or idle animations against footage in this pass. Capture them from the remake before animating.

## 3. Visual design

I viewed the official renders directly (1996 CG, 2023 key art, 2023 model sheet) and the SNES sprite sheet.

**Silhouette.** A head-heavy doll roughly 3 heads tall **[estimate]**, with a big floppy cap, a wide flared collar, a jagged cape and oversized shoes. At small sizes it reads by the hat, the collar and the shoes.

- **Hat.** A soft blue stocking cap with a floppy point. In 1996 the point droops backward; in 2023 it is felt and folds over. On the front is a gold emblem shaped like a ribbon bow, with two loops. **The hat is blue, not brown**; the brown is the shoes.
- **Hair.** Two orange curls, like wood shavings, poke out from under the hat over his right eye. The wiki calls them "two orange stripes".
- **Face.** Carved wood with a vertical seam down the middle, a wedge nose and a puppet-style hinged jaw (the manga is noted for dropping "the puppet design" of his mouth). Heavy-lidded, half-closed eyes with **red irises in 1996 and dark brown in 2023**. Round ears.
- **Cape and collar.** A blue cape with a zig-zag, tattered hem and a yellow lining. The lining flips up into a **wide, pointed yellow collar** that frames the head. A gold cord between two gold grommets fastens it at the throat.
- **Body.** Bare wood, with visible grain in 2023. A round, barrel-shaped torso and segmented, ball-jointed limbs with ring joints at the wrists, elbows, knees and ankles. Mitten hands with separate fingers.
- **Shoes.** Large, round, dark-brown clogs with ring cuffs.
- **Rendering.** 1996 is glossy pre-rendered CG: saturated royal blue, pale glossy wood, red eyes, sharp cape points. 2023 is plush felt with real wood grain and a lighter cerulean blue.

## 4. Sound and music

All music is by **Yoko Shimomura**, with some arrangements of Koji Kondo and Nobuo Uematsu themes. She returned for the remake's orchestral arrangements.

| Track (JP) | Where it plays | Use for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Beware of Forest Mushrooms** (森のキノコにご用心) | Forest Maze, where Geno is first met and fights Bowyer | **The Geno leitmotif**, used by Nintendo in both costume trailers (2015, 2020). It is in C♯ Dorian / F♯ Dorian, 4/4. Shimomura calls it "strangely lyrical", written to express "an eerie forest shrouded in mystery". |
| **Geno's Awakening** (ジーノの目覚め) | The cutscene where the star possesses the doll | Opening for the trailer's possession beat |
| **Farewell, Geno... / The Wishes from the Stars** (さよならジーノ…〜星の窓から見る夢は) | Geno leaves to repair the Star Road, then the ending | An emotional button or a victory sting |
| **Play "Save the World" with Me!** | Gaz's doll play (an arrangement of the *Super Mario World* Ground BGM) | A comic cold open |
| **Where Flowers Bloom under Starlight** | Star Hill, where wishes land | A starry mood |
| **Battling a Weapon Boss** | Smithy Gang bosses, including Bowyer | A fight cue |

The brief mentioned "Star Road/Seaside" themes. The sources don't tie a Seaside Town track to Geno, and there is no Star Road track as such; the Star Hill theme is the closest.

**Sound effects.** Little is documented. Verified: SMRPG's timed-hit success chime is recognisable enough that Legacy XP reused it ("a unique chiming sound that veterans of Super Mario RPG will surely recognize"), and Geno Beam's timed release adds no extra sound. Wooden clacks, joint creaks and star twinkles are **not documented** as canon Geno sounds; they are our design **[inference]** from the material. Record the star-meter tick, chime, charge hum and cannon transformation from both games directly.

## 5. Fan expectations

**Sample (small but consistent).** Legacy XP (the most-played real implementation); two Fantendo fan-game designs, *Global Apocalypse* and *Calamity*; two more fan pages (*Emerald*, *Worlds Collide*) saying only that every SMRPG move is a special and that his up-B is new, based on the cannon form; the Ultimate skin that puts Geno Whirl on Mega Man's Metal Blade; and Nintendo's costume (arm cannon on the right hand). We couldn't reach Reddit or SmashBoards, and the session's web-search budget ran out before YouTube concept videos.

| Slot | Most common choice | Alternatives seen | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral B | **Geno Beam**, charged, with 3 stars | Geno Whirl (*Calamity*; the Ultimate skin on Metal Blade) | LXP, Global Apocalypse |
| Side B | **Geno Whirl**, piercing, with a timed crit or detonation | Elbow or Hand Cannon shots (*Calamity*) | LXP, Global Apocalypse |
| Up B | **Cannon form fires himself** at an aimed angle | Geno Boost as a rocket (*Global Apocalypse*); cannon fires a star and he teleports to it (*Calamity*) | LXP, Calamity, Emerald |
| Down B | **Geno Blast**, stars or beams from above | Geno Boost buff for attack, knockback resistance or mobility (*Calamity*) | LXP, Global Apocalypse |
| Final Smash | **Geno Flash** (cannon and sun), in every design sampled | none | LXP, Global Apocalypse, Calamity |
| Signature mechanic | **Timed hits** | Only the Whirl is timed (*Calamity*) | LXP (on every aerial and smash) |
| Normals | Arm-cannon shots, rocket fists, finger bullets | — | LXP (explosive forward-air shot) |
| Flavour | "I serve a higher authority" crossed-arms taunt; head spins 360°; the star leaves and the doll drops limp; the entrance is a lifeless doll that the star descends into | — | Global Apocalypse |

**What fans want, with evidence.** A real fighter, not a costume (Newsweek, ScreenRant, the ballots). A faithful doll model: the most-liked Ultimate Geno mods are models, SeanHicksArt's Deluxe Mii costume (289 likes) and the model over Mega Man (204). Also the real SMRPG move names, Geno Flash as the finisher, timed hits, and the Forest Maze music.

**Dislikes.** The one well evidenced is the Mii costume as a substitute. Our own view **[inference]**: the biggest design risk is making him "a generic arm-cannon zoner", a Samus or Mii Gunner reskin that loses the doll and the timing.

**Legacy XP's Geno** is the design to know (from its archived official page):
- **Timed hits** on aerials and smashes: press just before contact for a stronger move, sometimes with different properties. Success shows four rainbow stars and the SMRPG chime; too early flashes yellow with a tone, too late flashes red with a lower beep.
- **Neutral B, Geno Beam:** charge up to three stars shown over his head, store the charge with shield, hidden timed hit.
- **Side B, Geno Whirl:** thrown at an angle, pierces and ricochets; a second press detonates it (the timed hit); it can be "disarmed".
- **Up B:** the cannon form rotates and fires Geno in many directions. No special fall, but he loses his double jump and airdodge.
- **Down B, Geno Blast:** 1–4 falling stars by charge, with specks of light marking where they'll land.
- **Final Smash:** Geno Flash. Framed as a glass cannon with below-par recovery.

We found no reception data beyond it staying in LXP builds through 2019 and being ported into later builds.

**Novelty verdict for Melee.** No Geno in GameBanana's Melee section, in SmashWiki's list of Melee mods (Akaneia Build, Beyond Melee, Turbo DX, ACE Build), or in the ACE Build public repo's 54 fighter slots, and ACE aims to collect every community character. SmashBoards and Discord went unchecked. **Conclusion: a Melee Geno probably doesn't exist publicly (medium confidence).** But the "faithful" layout is already LXP's in any Smash. To stand apart without losing fidelity: a new home for Geno Flash (Melee has no Final Smash anyway), a stronger doll identity (detachable parts, the limp-doll star exit, the cannon transformation), and timing that feels unlike LXP's **[inference]**.
